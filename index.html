Every figure here is an estimate

Nothing in this app is connected to payroll or to HMRC. It works entirely off what you type into it, so it can only be as right as what it has been told.

Four things make it closer, in the order they are worth doing:

  • Setup → Pay so far this tax year. The biggest one by far. Take the year-to-date boxes off your latest payslip, or off a P45. Without it the app guesses your tax from the weeks it can see, and early on that is not many.
  • Setup → your tax code, off a real payslip. It assumes the standard code until you tell it otherwise, and it cannot check the one you type.
  • Setup → rates, student loan, Medicash, pension. They are all editable because not everyone is on the same ones.
  • The Payslip tab, once a slip actually lands. Put the real numbers in and it will tell you where it differs.

This is not payroll and it is not tax advice. Your payslip is the real answer. If the two disagree, it is worth asking payroll — but check here first, because the app may simply not know something yet.
